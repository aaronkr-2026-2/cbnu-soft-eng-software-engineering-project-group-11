## Getting help

Include your FiLog version/build, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, the action that failed and whether it happens offline. For sync issues, include the status shown in FiLog Settings and whether Photos access is limited or full. Remove private notes, locations, account details and images from screenshots before sending them. Do not send passwords or your entire library.

## Photos are missing

FiLog links to originals in Apple Photos. Check that the original still exists and that FiLog has permission to access it. In FiLog Settings, use the Photos access/repair tools or relink an unavailable frame. Matching photos on another device also depends on iCloud Photos and available identifiers. Granting permission alone does not guarantee recovery.

## Sync or restore is pending

Check your Apple Account, iCloud availability/storage and network, then reopen FiLog and review sync status. Previously synced metadata may be recoverable with the same Apple Account. FiLog does not back up original photo files, app-owned photo copies or custom cover images through its metadata sync. A synced metadata status does not mean every photo is accessible.

**Do not uninstall FiLog, clear app storage or delete iCloud data to troubleshoot.** Those actions can lose local-only files. Contact support first if your existing library is at risk. Do not alter the separate FILMER app or its cloud data.

## Privacy and data controls

Use FiLog Settings to read the in-app privacy explanation, manage permissions and control sync. Deleting a FiLog frame does not delete its Apple Photos original. Synced deletions can affect other FiLog devices. Turning sync off does not erase existing cloud data.
