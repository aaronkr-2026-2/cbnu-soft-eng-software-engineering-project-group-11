
# User Personas – FiLog

## Project Overview

FiLog is a mobile application designed for film photography enthusiasts. It helps users organize their film photographs into digital albums, record film roll information, and easily revisit their photography memories.

The goal of the MVP is to provide a simple and intuitive way to manage film photography collections.

---

## Persona 1: Minh – Experienced Film Photographer

### Personal
- **Name:** Minh Nguyen
- **Age:** 24
- **Situation:** Master's student who has been practicing film photography for several years.

### Job / Daily Activities
- Studies at university and takes film photographs during free time.
- Uses different film cameras and film stocks.
- Develops and scans film rolls at photography labs.
- Organizes scanned photographs on his phone and computer.

### Education / Technical Skills
- Comfortable using smartphones and photography software.
- Understands film formats, ISO, and camera settings.
- Prefers simple and efficient interfaces.

### Relevance
Minh has hundreds of scanned film photographs stored in different folders. It is difficult to remember which camera or film stock was used for each roll. He needs an application to organize his photographs and film information in one place.

### User Stories
1. As an experienced film photographer, I want to create separate albums for each film roll so that I can organize my photographs.
2. As a film photographer, I want to record film stock and camera information so that I can remember how each roll was shot.
3. As a film photographer, I want to view my photographs by album so that I can easily revisit previous shoots.

---

## Persona 2: Anna – Beginner Film Photographer

### Personal
- **Name:** Anna Kim
- **Age:** 21
- **Situation:** University student who recently started exploring film photography.

### Job / Daily Activities
- Attends university classes.
- Takes photographs of friends, cafes, and everyday life.
- Uses a simple 35mm film camera.
- Receives digital scans from a film lab.

### Education / Technical Skills
- Familiar with common smartphone applications.
- Has basic knowledge of photography.
- Prefers a clean interface without complicated technical settings.

### Relevance
Anna is new to film photography and wants to keep her scanned photographs organized. She sometimes forgets which photographs belong to which film roll. She needs an easy way to create albums and save her memories.

### User Stories
1. As a beginner film photographer, I want to create a film album easily so that I can organize my first film rolls.
2. As a beginner, I want to add photographs from my phone to an album so that I can keep related pictures together.
3. As a beginner, I want to view my photographs in a simple gallery so that I can enjoy my film photography memories.

---

## Persona 3: Daniel – Casual Film Photography Enthusiast

### Personal
- **Name:** Daniel Lee
- **Age:** 29
- **Situation:** Office worker who enjoys film photography during weekends and vacations.

### Job / Daily Activities
- Works full-time at a company.
- Takes film photographs while traveling.
- Uses film cameras to capture special moments.
- Stores scanned photographs in his phone's photo library.

### Education / Technical Skills
- Comfortable with everyday mobile applications.
- Has intermediate photography knowledge.
- Values convenience and quick access to photographs.

### Relevance
Daniel takes photographs during trips but often mixes his film scans with regular smartphone photos. He wants to organize his film photographs into meaningful collections so that he can revisit his travel memories.

### User Stories
1. As a casual film photographer, I want to organize photographs into albums so that I can separate different trips and film rolls.
2. As a traveler, I want to give each album a name and date so that I can remember when the photographs were taken.
3. As a film photography enthusiast, I want to browse my film albums so that I can quickly find my favorite memories.

## MVP Features Based on User Needs

| Feature | Related Personas | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Create film albums | Minh, Anna, Daniel | Organize photographs by film roll |
| Import photographs | Minh, Anna, Daniel | Add scanned film photos from the phone |
| Record film information | Minh, Anna | Remember camera and film stock details |
| Album name and date | Minh, Daniel | Identify and organize photography sessions |
| Album gallery | Minh, Anna, Daniel | Browse photographs in an organized way |
| Simple user interface | Anna, Daniel | Make the app easy to use |

## Conclusion

These three personas represent different levels of film photography experience: experienced, beginner, and casual users.

Although their photography habits are different, they share a common need: organizing scanned film photographs in a simple and convenient way.

Therefore, the FiLog MVP focuses on album creation, photo importing, film information management, and photo browsing. These features address the core needs of the target users without adding unnecessary complexity.
